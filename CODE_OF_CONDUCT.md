# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Freebots a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming language, respecting viewpoints, accepting feedback gracefully,
focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes: harassment, trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, publishing others'
private information without permission, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in warning, temporary ban, or permanent removal from project spaces at the maintainers'
discretion. Report issues privately to the repository maintainers with a description and any relevant links —
do not include sensitive personal data beyond what is necessary.
